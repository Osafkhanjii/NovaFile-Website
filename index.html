Why NovaFile

Built around how you actually work

Every feature below is in the current release — no roadmap promises.

Ctrl+N · Ctrl+Tab

Folder tabs

Keep many folders open at once, reorder them, close them individually or all at once — up to 20 tabs per window.

Ctrl+E · Ctrl+W

Split views

Divide the window into panes and work in two folders side by side. Layouts can nest, and each pane remembers its own path.

Drag a tab

Drag-and-drop docking

Drag a tab over a pane and drop zones preview exactly where it will land. Rearrange your layout without touching a menu.

Ctrl+F

Search & navigation

Filter the current folder as you type, press Enter to scan subfolders with progress, and jump anywhere from the address bar.

Ctrl+,

Keyboard shortcuts

Every core action is a keystroke, and shortcuts are rebindable in Settings — with per-shortcut on/off switches.

F3 · Ctrl+T

Views & themes

Five view modes with size zoom from 15–100%, crisp shell thumbnails, and dark or light themes.
